Our journey

The legacy of Vikram Brothers, carried forward into a new tea chapter.

From a small retail outlet in Gujarat to a broader tea trade presence in Mumbai and beyond, the story of Namo 9 is one of persistence, trust, and growth.

Milestones

Great tea businesses are built one chapter at a time.

1963

First retail beginnings

Shri Kanubhai Parekh entered the tea industry with a retail outlet in Gujarat, laying the groundwork for a long-standing family trade.

1975

Wholesale expansion

As demand grew, the business moved deeper into wholesale trade and broadened its market reach.

1985

Expanding into Mumbai

The wholesale chain expanded further, creating a strong foothold in Mumbai and strengthening the business in Maharashtra.

Late 1980s

Legacy into the next generation

Mr. Nilesh Parekh developed expertise in tea purchasing and tasting, built relationships with brokers, and introduced customized tea packs.

Today

Modern brand, same values

Mr. Meet Parekh now carries the business forward through the new brand, Namo 9 Tea, focusing on expansion, new product lines, and modern presentation.

The modern chapter

Honouring tradition while bringing the brand into the future.

Today, the business is not limited to Mumbai. It reaches buyers across the domestic market and has also entered international space. The next chapter is defined by new ideas, broader offerings, and an even richer tea experience.

  • Certified tea tasting expertise informing every blend.
  • New product lines designed for contemporary tea drinkers.
  • Modern storytelling without losing the heritage behind each leaf.
