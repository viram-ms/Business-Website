Since 1984

The Legacy of Vikram Brothers

1963

Before starting the journey of Vikram Brothers, Shri Kanubhai Parekh entered into the Tea Industry with a retail outlet from Gujarat, India — laying the foundation of a legacy that would span three generations.

1975

With increasing demands, Shri Kanubhai Parekh also entered the wholesale market, expanding the business beyond retail and establishing strong trade relationships across Gujarat.

1985

He decided to expand the wholesale chain along with retail into Mumbai, capturing the Maharashtra market and establishing Vikram Brothers as a name synonymous with quality Indian tea.

Today

Mr. Nilesh Parekh continued the legacy by developing expertise in purchasing and tasting teas, creating strong relations with brokers and retailers, and producing customized packets for consumers. Today the sale of Vikram Brothers is not only in Mumbai but is present all over the domestic market.

Now, Mr. Meet Parekh has taken the responsibility of expanding Vikram Brothers into the new brand of “NAMO 9” Tea, reaching new heights. He introduced a variety of teas and plans on expanding the product line. Today the company is not only doing business in the domestic market but has also entered the international market. He is also a certified Tea Taster.

Premium Indian tea since 1984. Three generations of expertise, delivering the finest teas from across India.
