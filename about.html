Our Story

Why Choose Us…

Welcome to the world of NAMO 9 where everyday is a fresh start to all the new opportunities and beginnings with our special blend of pure Assam Tea. Our teas are blended by tea experts and tea tasters who are devoted to ensure that the tea leaves are of its best possible quality.

With three generations in the Tea Industry we provide you the best of Indian Teas in domestic as well as in international markets (exports). With over 50 years of existence in the tea industry, we have earned the reputation of offering best qualities of Indian teas. We started the business in Gujarat as retailers, after establishing the base over there we expanded into wholesale. Later we started working in Mumbai and captured market in the state of Maharashtra, India. Our team of experienced people will be with you throughout the selection process and will help you to blend the right teas to meet your requirements.
