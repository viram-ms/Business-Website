Packaging

Flexible packaging solutions that suit bulk trade and private-label brands.

We provide packaging in formats that match your product needs, whether you need large bulk packs or retail-ready pouches with your own brand identity.

Bulk packing

Reliable bulk packaging for large-volume business needs.

Bulk packing is available in kraft paper, paper bags with food-grade quality, jute bags, or HDPE bags. Each bag contains between 20 and 40 kilograms depending on the tea type.

  • Food-grade materials available for safe storage and transport.
  • Flexible options for different tea grades and volumes.
  • Built for wholesalers, retailers, and export buyers.

Private labeling

Custom tea packs designed around your brand identity.

If you want tea packets under your own brand name, we can create them in varying weights and formats, from 250 grams to 1 kilogram, in stand-up pouches, poly pouches, mono cartons, PET jars, or 5-kilogram jute and non-woven bags.

  • Flexible weights and pack sizes tailored to your requirement.
  • Food-grade materials across multiple pack formats.
  • Ready for private-label retail, wholesale, or export channels.
