Wholesale

Bulk Packing

Bulk packing can be provided by us in all form and size. This is made available in kraft paper/paper bag (food grade quality), jute bag or HDPE bag. Each bag contains from 20 to 40 kgs depending on the types of tea leaves.

Custom Branding

Private Labeling

If you want tea packets in your own brand name, feel free to contact us. This is done in various weights according to requirements of the clients. The material used will be food grade quality from 250 gms to 1 kg in standup pouch, poly pouch, mono cartons, PET Jars or 5kg in jute/Non woven bag.
